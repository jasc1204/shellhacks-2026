# 3-minute demo script

Round 1 is one judge for 3 minutes. Open the map before they arrive, set to **MAP + 2D** with nothing selected, and keep the 3D world open in a second tab.

| Time | Do | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Map overview | "Dominion Energy in South Carolina and Georgia Power plan their transmission work years ahead, separately. In 2024 FERC told utilities to plan together. Sperry asked: where exactly would that pay off?" |
| 0:20 | Point at the header numbers | "I read both public plans, 252 projects, and put 217 on the map. Every one links back to its page in the filing. OpenStreetMap doesn't name every substation, so I traced the power lines to find the missing ones." |
| 0:45 | Click **#1** in the list | "They're ranked the way Sperry's spec asks: distance first, then timing. #1 is at Thurmond Dam on the Savannah River. DESC's Hooks–Thurmond tie and Georgia Power's two Evans–Thurmond Dam lines end in **the same substation**. They touch, so the two utilities have to coordinate outages there. As planned, their build windows are **4.4 years apart**, and lining them up is the opportunity." |
| 1:10 | Toggle **CENTERS**, then back | "It's also the first overlap in Sperry's own sample. Their starter guide measures center to center, which makes it look about 4 miles apart. The spec says closest points: they share the same yard." |
| 1:30 | Click **#3** | "The best pair to share as planned: DESC's new $23.8M Jasper–Okatie line and Georgia Power's McIntosh–Purrysburg work, **0.94 km apart**, both under construction for **the same 24 months**. That's one crew mobilization and shared land instead of two, roughly **$1M**, and every assumption is editable." |
| 1:55 | Press **▶ TOUR** (let it fly to the first stop) | "Here's the same place on satellite imagery in 3D." (press → once to show the next one) |
| 2:10 | Esc, then **View in 3D** on #3 | "And this is the whole border as a 3D world, built from the same data." (fly or walk for 10 s) |
| 2:25 | Back on the map: **+ ADD A PROJECT** | "It's a tool, not just a report. Say Georgia Power plans a new line here…" (click two points, SCORE IT) "…and it instantly shows which DESC projects it would collide with." |
| 2:50 | Close | "78 overlaps, 34 happening at the same time, all checked against Sperry's own sample. Next: real line routes and more utilities." |

## If you reach Round 2 (3–5 min)

- **Data Quality tab:**
  - All 6 of Sperry's sample overlaps are reproduced, with dates matching to the day.
  - Three DESC budget tables don't add up in the source PDF.
  - Every manual location cites its evidence.
- **Filters:** click **BOTH** to keep only the pairs that are close and timed together. Everything else dims.
- **Walk the gap** in the 3D world for #3: stand at the DESC end and look at Georgia Power's line.
- **GOOGLE EARTH** on #1: the shared substation at Thurmond Dam in Google's 3D, one click from the card. The **GOOGLE 3D** switch in the 3D world does the same inside our world. Both need Wi-Fi.
- **Downloads:** the overlap table in Sperry's own spreadsheet columns, and the Google Earth file with every project and overlap: "a planner can open this in the tools they already use."

## Before judging

1. Delete any test projects under **Your projects**.
2. Close other tabs and apps, and plug in your charger.
3. Test the Wi-Fi. If it's bad, satellite tiles load slowly, so demo in **MAP** mode and use the 3D world, which loads its own files.
4. Be at your table by 1:00 PM Sunday.
